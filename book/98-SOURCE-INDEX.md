Reading estimates below were recomputed from the revised page-text snapshots, not inferred from PDF page count. ## Suggested reading order
### Fast interview loop
1. Start with [00\_05\_for\_interview\_candidates](front-matter/00_05_for_interview_candidates.md) and [01\_what\_a\_rag\_interview\_actually\_tests](part-01-landscape/01_what_a_rag_interview_actually_tests.md) for the source’s practice rubric.
2. Read [03\_a\_repeatable\_framework\_for\_any\_rag\_design\_question](part-01-landscape/03_a_repeatable_framework_for_any_rag_design_question.md) for a useful design lens, not an exhaustive free-parameter theorem.
3. Read Chapters 15 through 18 for search, scoped memory, operations and classical IR.
4. Read Chapters 21, 22, 24, 25 and 26 for hybrid retrieval, reranking, reformulation, routing and loops.
5. Read Chapters 30 through 34 for context, support and diagnostic tests allowing multiple causes.
6. Read Chapters 35 through 38 for credibility, provenance, systems and federation.
7. Finish with [41\_end\_to\_end\_design\_drills](part-12-systems-design/41_end_to_end_design_drills.md) and Appendices A1, A2, A3 and A6. This broad route is distinct from the narrower one-week plan and is not a readiness guarantee.
### Complete technical path
1. Read front matter units $`00.01`$ through $`00.09`$ for scope, navigation, notation, and study method.
2. Read Chapters 1 through 14 for design framing, generator behavior, prompting, chunking, parsing, and embedding.
3. Read Chapters 15 through 29 for indexes, retrieval, ranking, reformulation, control, and training data.
4. Read Chapters 30 through 41 for context, attribution, evaluation, credibility, adversarial robustness, systems, federation, multimodality, graphs, and design drills.
5. Use Appendices A1 through A7 as formula, practice, checklist, reporting, reading, notation, and glossary references.
### Production system path
1. Begin with Chapters 3, 12, 13 and 14 for framing, representation, retrieval units and parsing.
2. Continue with Chapters 15, 16, 17, 21 and 22 for capacity and serving trade-offs.
3. Add Chapters 24, 25, 26 and 30 for query/control and context decisions.
4. Validate with Chapters 31 through 38 and [A4\_the\_rag\_card\_and\_index\_datasheet](appendices/A4_the_rag_card_and_index_datasheet.md).
5. Add Chapters 39 and 40 when modality, graph structure, provenance or global-query requirements justify them.
6. Use [41\_end\_to\_end\_design\_drills](part-12-systems-design/41_end_to_end_design_drills.md) and [A3\_design\_checklists](appendices/A3_design_checklists.md) for practice and review, then complete workload-specific tests and release requirements. Reading the route does not itself approve deployment.
### Evaluation and trust path
1. Read Chapters 5, 10 and 11 for data/legal boundaries, prompt sensitivity, abstention and calibration.
2. Read Chapters 27 through 29 for generator/retriever training and synthetic-data limitations.
3. Read Chapters 31 through 36 for citation support, retrieval/generation metrics, end-to-end tests, credibility, provenance and attacks.
4. Read Chapters 37 and 38 for operational work, cost and distributed governance.
5. Complete [A4\_the\_rag\_card\_and\_index\_datasheet](appendices/A4_the_rag_card_and_index_datasheet.md) using measured artifacts, explicit uncertainty and domain-specific review; this is not a universal compliance certification.
